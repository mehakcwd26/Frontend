Welcome to ReuseLoop

ReuseLoop is a student-to-student reuse platform designed to help college students donate, sell, exchange and borrow useful items.

Our mission is to reduce unnecessary waste, save students' money and create a sustainable college community.

About ReuseLoop

Our Problem

Students often have unused books, clothes, bags and electronics while other students need those same items.

Our Solution

ReuseLoop connects students to donate, sell, exchange or borrow reusable items.

Our Mission

Reduce waste, save money and promote sustainability in college communities.

How ReuseLoop Works

1. List an Item

Add the item name, category and description.

2. Find an Item

Explore categories and search for useful items.

3. Contact Owner

Contact the owner and arrange a safe exchange.

Technology Used

HTML

Creates the website structure.

CSS

Designs the website interface.

JavaScript

Handles navigation, search and listings.

Future Scope

Firebase Authentication, shared database, college verification, notifications and item availability tracking.
